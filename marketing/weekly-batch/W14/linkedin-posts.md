## Post 2: Story/Opinion
Over two decades of commissioning systems at Konark Process Instruments, I have learned that the success of a project is often decided long before it reaches the plant floor. It is decided in the panel fabrication shop.

I often tell engineering heads that a control panel is the central nervous system of their process. Poor wiring standards, missing labels, and cramped layouts do not just look unprofessional -- they create nightmares during commissioning and maintenance. When an operator has to troubleshoot a tripped circuit at 2 AM, clear ferruling and logical wire routing save hours of downtime.

We refuse to compromise on fabrication discipline. Every panel we build is subjected to strict quality checks, standardized wire routing, and comprehensive documentation. It takes more effort upfront, but it ensures that when our panels arrive on site, they run right the first time.
