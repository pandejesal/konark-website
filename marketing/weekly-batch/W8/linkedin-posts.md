## Post 2: Trust/Story (Bharat Forge)

**Headline:** Synchronizing burner safety with material handling on a continuous line

A continuous heat-treatment furnace cannot tolerate loose control. If the temperature drifts or the material handling pauses unexpectedly, the metallurgical cycle is broken.

When we approached the automation for the Bharat Forge continuous heat-treatment furnace line, we knew burner safety and line automation had to operate as a single unified system. A Burner Management System alone was not enough; the thermal control had to be synchronized precisely with the material movement.

We delivered an integrated solution. We engineered the burner management system alongside the production line automation. Under one control philosophy, the safety interlocks communicate directly with the production sequencing.

The result is a heat-treatment line where the burners maintain safe, consistent thermal profiles while the material handling keeps everything moving in cycle. It is the type of heavy industry engineering that demands process understanding before writing a single line of PLC logic.
