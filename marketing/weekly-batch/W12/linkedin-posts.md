## Post 1 (Educational)

A furnace startup sequence is where hidden control logic flaws usually reveal themselves. The process requires careful purging, proving, and ignition timing.

If your Burner Management System relies on poorly timed logic or bypassed purge cycles to save a few minutes, you are introducing significant operational risk. A safe startup sequence must strictly follow NFPA or EN standards to ensure no unburned combustibles remain in the chamber before ignition.

At Konark Process Instruments, I design every BMS with rigorous startup sequences hardcoded into the logic. This means purge times are enforced, flame scanning is verified, and the system fails to a safe state if any condition is not met. Proper engineering upfront prevents dangerous accumulations and protects your equipment from catastrophic events.
