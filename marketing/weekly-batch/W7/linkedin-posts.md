## Post 1: Educational (Retrofitting Furnace Automation)

**Headline:** Retrofitting Furnace Automation: You Do Not Need to Rebuild the Furnace

**Body:**
When plant managers look at an aging heat-treatment or galvanising furnace, the assumption is often that improving thermal control requires a complete mechanical rebuild. In our experience at Konark Process Instruments, we have found this is rarely the case.

You can retrofit modern automation into existing furnace lines without rebuilding the physical structure. The mechanical components of a furnace typically outlast their original control systems by decades. By focusing on the logic, we update the automation layer to synchronize thermal control with the material-handling sequence.

This means implementing zone-wise temperature control with precise thermal profiling for consistent metallurgy, all controlled via modern PLC and SCADA systems. We replace outdated relay logic with documented, tested interlocks and an operator-friendly HMI. The result is a furnace that runs repeatably, shift after shift, without the capital expenditure of a full mechanical replacement.

If your furnace hardware is sound but your control logic is failing, an automation retrofit is the practical engineering solution.

## Post 2: Trust/Story (Jindal Saw Case Study)

**Headline:** Managing Self-Recuperative Burners at Jindal Saw

**Body:**
Reheating furnaces in the steel industry burn fuel around the clock. When exhaust heat leaves the stack, it is a direct financial loss for the plant. Managing recuperative burners safely to recover that heat is a specialist skill we have refined over many years.

At Konark Process Instruments, we delivered self-recuperative burner management for Jindal Saw. The challenge with waste-heat recovery is that using exhaust gas to preheat combustion air introduces complex control variables. Our solution was to engineer burner control that managed this preheating process alongside the strict safety interlocks that a recuperative system demands.

The control logic we implemented ensures that energy savings from recovered waste heat are achieved without ever compromising burner safety or furnace stability. In industrial automation, efficiency cannot come at the expense of reliable, safe operation. The system must fail safe, and it must keep the furnace in cycle.

If you are looking to improve the efficiency of your reheating furnaces, let us discuss the control logic required.
