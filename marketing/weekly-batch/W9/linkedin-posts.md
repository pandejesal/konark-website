## Post 2: Trust/Story (Vesuvius India)

**Headline:** Holding the temperature line in continuous casting

In continuous casting, the tundish is the last defense for steel quality before the mold. If the heating drifts during the casting sequence, you are left with downstream quality issues and wasted gas.

When we engineered the Dumac system for tundish heating for Vesuvius India, we focused on precision. Standard automation is not enough here; the temperature control must be exact and repeatable through the entire sequence.

We designed the burner management and temperature control explicitly around the casting process. The logic ensures that the heat remains steady, providing dependable tundish temperature control without burning fuel needlessly.

It is specialized thermal engineering, and it is the foundation of our tundish heating service today. When the process demands precise, repeatable heating, the control logic must be built for that specific task.
