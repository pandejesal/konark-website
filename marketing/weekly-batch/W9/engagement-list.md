# Week 9 Engagement List

1. **ABB India**
   - **Why relevant:** A major player in industrial automation and power. Engaging here builds credibility in the broader automation ecosystem.
   - **Comment angle:** Discuss how robust component selection and in-house panel testing are non-negotiable for systems exposed to high heat and vibration in continuous process industries.

2. **L&T Electrical & Automation**
   - **Why relevant:** Key supplier and manufacturer in India's industrial sector.
   - **Comment angle:** Focus on the importance of clear, as-built documentation and clean panel wiring to support long-term maintenance and minimize downtime.

3. **Indian Institute of Metals (IIM)**
   - **Why relevant:** Connects directly to the metallurgical aspects of Konark's work (e.g., Bharat Forge, Vesuvius, Jindal Saw).
   - **Comment angle:** Highlight the critical link between precise thermal control (BMS, tundish heating) and maintaining consistent metallurgical quality in continuous casting and heat treatment.

4. **Heat Treat Today**
   - **Why relevant:** A global hub for heat-treating professionals, directly overlapping with Konark's core furnace automation services.
   - **Comment angle:** Share insights on synchronizing zone-wise temperature control with material handling sequencing to ensure repeatable thermal profiles in heat-treatment lines.

5. **Cement Manufacturers' Association (CMA) India**
   - **Why relevant:** Cement plants require heavy-duty burner management and general industrial automation under harsh conditions.
   - **Comment angle:** Emphasize the necessity of fail-safe burner logic and rigorous safety interlocks when handling dual-fuel systems in large-scale kilns.
