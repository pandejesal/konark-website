## Post 1 (Educational: Alarm management and alarm rationalization on HMI and SCADA)

An HMI flooded with alarms is a safety hazard, not a monitoring tool. When operators are overwhelmed by nuisance alarms, critical warnings are missed.

Effective SCADA design requires strict alarm rationalization. Every alarm configured in the PLC must be actionable, prioritized, and relevant to the current process state. We design our control interfaces to guide operators toward resolving issues, rather than forcing them to acknowledge a barrage of expected operational state changes. Clear, prioritized alarm management reduces operator fatigue and drastically improves response times during critical events.
