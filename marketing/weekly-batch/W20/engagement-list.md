# Week 20 Engagement List

## Prospects
1. **Target:** Operations Manager at an industrial bakery or food processing plant in Africa (similar to Kenblest).
   **Comment Angle:** Discuss the importance of having the original design engineers physically present for site commissioning and handover.
2. **Target:** Control Room Supervisor at a large manufacturing facility in India.
   **Comment Angle:** Ask about their strategies for managing alarm priorities during startup sequences to prevent operator overload.

## Industry Pages / Groups
3. **Target:** HMI and SCADA Design Professionals.
   **Comment Angle:** Share insight on how implementing ISA-18.2 standards for alarm management directly improves plant safety.
4. **Target:** International Engineering Commissioning Group.
   **Comment Angle:** Note that successful overseas commissioning requires designing for local electrical and environmental conditions from day one.
5. **Target:** Industrial Automation and Control Systems.
   **Comment Angle:** Highlight that an alarm should require a specific operator action; if it doesn't, it is an event, not an alarm.
