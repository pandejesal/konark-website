## Post 1 (Educational: Combustion efficiency and air-fuel ratio control)

When we evaluate industrial burner systems, the air-fuel ratio is the first metric that dictates long-term operating costs.

Achieving combustion efficiency is not just about basic ignition. It requires continuous control of excess air and dynamic oxygen trim. A system that drifts burns fuel needlessly and expels wasted heat up the stack. Our approach ensures that the control loop responds in real-time, matching fuel input to load demand while keeping excess air within tight tolerances. Proper air-fuel management protects the process and reduces waste.
