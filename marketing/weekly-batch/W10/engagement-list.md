# Week 10 Engagement List

1. **Schneider Electric India**
   - **Why relevant:** A major vendor for control panel components and switchgear used in Konark's custom fabrication.
   - **Comment angle:** Agree on posts highlighting panel safety or component durability, adding that selecting the right heavy-duty components is vital for control panels meant for continuous industrial use.

2. **Honeywell Industrial Automation India**
   - **Why relevant:** Leaders in process control and flame monitoring technology, overlapping directly with BMS engineering.
   - **Comment angle:** Discuss how advanced flame scanners (UV/IR) are only effective if the underlying PLC logic is programmed for fail-safe, rapid shutdown in continuous furnace operations.

3. **Association for Iron & Steel Technology (AIST)**
   - **Why relevant:** The premier technical association for steel manufacturing, covering applications like tundish heating and heat treatment.
   - **Comment angle:** Engage on articles about continuous casting efficiency, pointing out that dedicated automation for tundish heating is a proven method to reduce scrap and save gas.

4. **Indian Foundry Organization / The Institute of Indian Foundrymen (IIF)**
   - **Why relevant:** Foundries rely heavily on furnaces, making them a prime audience for automation retrofits.
   - **Comment angle:** Comment on the challenges of legacy equipment, explaining that intelligent retrofits of furnace control systems can drastically improve safety without rebuilding the entire hearth.

5. **The American Ceramic Society (ACerS)**
   - **Why relevant:** Ceramic production involves high-temperature kilns and furnaces that require exacting thermal profiles.
   - **Comment angle:** Share that understanding the specific metallurgical or chemical process cycle must always precede writing the automation logic for high-temperature kilns.
