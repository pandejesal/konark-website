## Post 1: Educational (Understanding Process Before Logic)

**Headline:** Why we study the furnace before we write a single line of logic

A burner, a flame scanner, and a thermocouple are just hardware. They only protect your plant when the right logic decides what to do with their signals.

In my practice, I have learned that you cannot automate what you do not understand. If an engineer writes PLC logic based solely on a generic template, the system will eventually fail to account for the unique dynamics of your thermal cycle.

Process understanding is the first step of every Konark project. Before we draft the burner management safety interlocks or sequence the material handling, we study your specific furnace, your burners, and your production cycle.

The logic we build must monitor flame, purge, and fuel, and it must shut everything down safely within seconds when something goes wrong. That requires a control philosophy engineered for your specific process, not just a product catalog.

## Post 2: Trust/Story (Kenblest Group, Kenya)

**Headline:** Why exporting automation means exporting the engineer

Shipping a control panel across borders is the easy part. Commissioning it overseas, ensuring it meets local conditions, and tuning it to international standards is where projects actually succeed or fail.

When we took on the burner systems commissioning for the Kenblest Group in Kenya, we did not just hand over drawings and walk away. Our model for overseas work is strict: engineer-led commissioning on site.

We traveled to Kenya to verify the installation, tune the burner systems, and execute the handover personally. We paid close attention to international compliance and ensured the local operators were trained on the exact as-built system.

The result is a fully operational burner system in Africa, proving that Konark automation travels. We support systems we built years ago, whether they are in Ahmedabad or across the world.
