## Post 1 (Educational: Thermocouples and temperature measurement reliability)

Accurate temperature measurement is the foundation of any reliable thermal control system. If your sensor data is wrong, your control logic will be wrong.

In industrial furnaces, selecting the correct thermocouple type for the process temperature and atmosphere is critical to prevent rapid sensor degradation. Over time, thermocouples experience drift, which can lead to off-spec production and increased fuel consumption. Regular calibration and understanding the limits of the sensor technology are non-negotiable for maintaining process integrity. We focus on engineering sensor networks that provide reliable, repeatable data to the PLC.
