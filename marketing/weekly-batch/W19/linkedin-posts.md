## Post 1 (Educational: Gas train design and safety shutoff valves)

A Burner Management System is only as safe as the gas train it controls. The design of the gas train dictates the physical safety of the entire combustion system.

We engineer gas trains with mandatory double block and bleed arrangements, ensuring absolute isolation when the burner is off. Valve proving and automated leak testing sequences must be integrated into the PLC logic before any ignition attempt. Cutting corners on safety shutoff valves or proving logic introduces unacceptable risks to the plant and personnel. Proper gas train engineering is a fundamental requirement, not an optional upgrade.
