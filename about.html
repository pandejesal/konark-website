About us

Two decades of automation that had to work

Our story

The company

For over 20 years, Konark Process Instruments has designed, built and commissioned the systems that keep industrial furnaces and burners running safely — from our engineering facility at Mahavir Industrial Park, Vatva, Ahmedabad, across India, and on overseas commissioning assignments worldwide.

We are a control engineering company, not a trading house: the PLC logic, the panel wiring, the safety interlocks and the commissioning are all done by our own engineers. That single-owner accountability is why our work appears aboard Indian Navy vessels and on the production lines of Bharat Forge — and why clients like Vesuvius India, Jindal Saw and the Kenblest Group in Kenya have trusted us on critical process work.

The founder

Mr. Nilesh Pande

Mr. Nilesh Pande is a veteran automation engineer and the founder and lead engineer of Konark. He specializes in Burner Management Systems and thermal process control, with hands-on expertise in PLC programming (Siemens, Rockwell), SCADA and industrial control panels.

His defining strength is the bridge between furnace hardware and control logic. A burner, a flame scanner, a thermocouple — each is just hardware until the right logic decides what to do with its signal. Mr. Pande builds that logic: the systems that monitor flame, purge and fuel, and shut everything down safely within seconds when something goes wrong. He reviews the engineering on every project. That is why our systems are built to fail safe — not just to run.

How we work

Approach & capability

Every project follows the same four steps, regardless of size:

  • Process understanding first — we study the furnace, the burners and the production cycle before writing a line of logic.
  • Engineering — documented logic design, panel engineering and component selection for continuous industrial duty.
  • Rigorous testing — panels tested against the approved logic in-house before they reach your plant.
  • Documentation and commissioning — drawings, manuals and as-built records you can hand to any maintenance team.

Quality and safety are not slogans here; they are the acceptance criteria for every deliverable, from a single panel to a complete furnace line.
