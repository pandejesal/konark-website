01

Burner Management Systems

A burner management system is a safety system first and an automation system second. We engineer BMS that supervise flame, fuel and purge conditions continuously, and take the burner to a safe state within seconds of any fault. Our systems are built for gas, oil and dual-fuel burners, on new installations and as retrofits, and integrate cleanly with self-recuperative burner technology for energy-efficient operation.

  • Safe start-up, flame monitoring and shutdown sequences with purge and leak-test interlocks
  • Flame supervision with UV/IR detection and fail-safe fuel valve logic
  • Integration with furnace control, SCADA and plant-level systems
  • Retrofits to existing burners with minimum production downtime

02

Industrial Furnace Automation

Furnaces are not just heated boxes — they are synchronized systems of temperature, atmosphere and material movement. We automate hearth, heat-treatment and galvanising furnaces, tying thermal control to the material-handling sequence so cycles run repeatably, shift after shift.

  • Zone-wise temperature control with thermal profiling for consistent metallurgy
  • Sequencing of charging, heating, soaking and discharge with safety interlocks
  • Retrofit automation into existing furnace lines without rebuilding the furnace
  • Operator-friendly HMI with alarms, trends and recipe management

05

Specialized Heating Systems

Where standard automation will not do, we build specialist thermal systems. Tundish heating for continuous casting demands precise, repeatable temperature control through the casting sequence; self-recuperative burners recover waste heat to preheat combustion air, cutting fuel consumption. Both are core Konark disciplines, proven in the field.

  • Tundish heating systems with precise temperature control for continuous casting
  • Self-recuperative burner management with waste-heat recovery
  • Combustion air preheating for measurable fuel savings on reheating furnaces
  • Engineering, panel build and on-site commissioning as one contract
