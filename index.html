Why Konark

The engineering behind the brand

Large industrial buyers do not buy automation on price. They buy on evidence of reliability. This is ours.

Safety-critical track record

We delivered DG-set automation for Indian Navy vessels INS Yamuna and INS Savitri. The same documented logic, tested interlocks and no-shortcut approach applies to every burner management system we build.

In-house panel fabrication

Control panels are designed, wired and tested under one roof at our Vatva, Ahmedabad facility. The team that draws the panel also wires it. Nothing gets lost between design and build.

PLC & SCADA depth

Hands-on programming across Siemens and Rockwell platforms, with SCADA visualization and real-time data acquisition built around your process.

A founder who stays involved

Mr. Nilesh Pande, founder and lead engineer, has spent over 20 years bridging furnace hardware with control logic. He reviews the engineering on every project.
