Indian Navy — DG-set automation

Defense · INS Yamuna & INS Savitri

Challenge

Power on a naval vessel must be reliable under hard conditions. Start, monitoring and shutdown cannot fail. Commercial-grade logic does not pass here.

Solution

DG-set automation engineered on INS Yamuna and INS Savitri with rigorous military safety and reliability standards — documented interlocks, thorough testing and disciplined commissioning.

Result

Automated DG sets operating reliably aboard both vessels. The discipline demanded by the Navy is now our default standard for every burner and furnace project.

Bharat Forge — BMS + production line automation

Heavy Industry · Continuous heat-treatment furnace

Challenge

A continuous heat-treatment furnace does not tolerate loose control: temperature must hold to cycle, and the production line must keep moving in sync. Burner safety and line automation had to work as one system.

Solution

Burner management system plus production line automation for the continuous heat-treatment furnace — thermal control and material-handling synchronization under one engineered control philosophy.

Result

An automated heat-treatment line where burner safety interlocks and production sequencing operate together, keeping the furnace safely in cycle.

Vesuvius India — Dumac system for tundish heating

Continuous Casting · Tundish heating

Challenge

In continuous casting, the tundish must hold steel at a precise temperature through the casting sequence. Heating that drifts means quality problems downstream — and burning gas needlessly.

Solution

Engineering of the Dumac system for tundish heating — burner management and precise temperature control designed around the casting process.

Result

Dependable tundish temperature control for continuous casting operations, the foundation of our specialized tundish heating service today.

Jindal Saw — self-recuperative burner management

Steel · Energy efficiency

Challenge

Reheating furnaces burn fuel around the clock. When exhaust heat leaves the stack, it is money leaving with it. Managing recuperative burners safely is a specialist skill.

Solution

Self-recuperative burner management — burner control engineered so waste-heat recovery preheats combustion air, with the safety interlocks a recuperative system demands.

Result

Energy savings from recovered waste heat, delivered without compromising burner safety or furnace stability.
